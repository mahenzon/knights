Defend your castle, fling the invaders, and come back for one more day.

A browser restoration of the 2009 castle-defense game, with its original artwork and soundtrack. Play with a mouse or touchscreen, then install it for offline adventures. No account needed.

The permanent play link will be added here when the site is published.

## Your first battle

1. On the game page, choose **Play → Campaign**.
2. If a saved battle is offered, choose **YES**, then **Resume** to pick up where you left off. Otherwise, select an unlocked castle and choose **Play**.
3. Grab an attacker, drag, and release to throw. Keep them away from your gates and spend your earnings on repairs and upgrades between battles.

Open **Help** from the main menu for illustrated controls and tips. On a phone or tablet, turn your device sideways for a larger battlefield.

Want a different challenge? Try **Endless Siege** or **Madness** from the Play menu. The game includes 12 castles, 193 campaign days, three difficulty levels, and 23 shop items.

## Install and play offline

Installation is optional—you can play in a browser tab.

1. Open the game while connected to the internet and wait for **Ready offline**. The full download is about **23 MB**.
2. Add the game to your device:
   - **Android:** in Chrome's menu, choose **Install** or **Add to Home screen**.
   - **iPhone or iPad:** in Safari, choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Leave **Open as Web App** enabled if offered.
3. Open the knight icon to play. Once the download is complete, you can play without an internet connection.

Installed offline play with sound has been checked on Android. iPhone and iPad offline play and audio are still being tested.

## Keep your progress

Your progress saves automatically in this browser on this device. Campaign, Endless Siege, and Madness each keep their own saved run.

For a break, tap **Pause** or press **Escape**. Switching tabs or apps also pauses and saves the battle. Choose **Resume** when you return. **Abort** ends the current run and removes its battle save.

Use the same browser and game address to keep playing your save. Saves do not sync between devices, and there is no export or backup feature yet. Clearing the site's browser data removes your progress; browsers may also remove stored data when space is low.

## Sound and updates

If the game is quiet, tap the screen once to allow sound. **Options** lets you adjust music volume or mute the music; this does not mute sound effects. For complete silence, use your device or browser's volume controls.

When **Update game** appears, tap it to load the new version. Your saved progress is kept. If a download is interrupted, reconnect and try again—the previous offline version stays available.

If the game will not load, check your connection and choose **Try again**. Avoid clearing browser data as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your saves.

## Original game and history

Original game, artwork, and soundtrack: **Moregames Entertainment / Chillingo**. This project restores the game for browsers without claiming ownership of the original resources or recovering the lost source code.

[Legacy Store](https://legacystore.app/app/312494232) records Top Paid positions of **#172 in Games** (11 January 2010), **#271 in Arcade** (14 April 2011), and **#356 in Action** (15 April 2011). These are the best positions in its limited archived captures, not confirmed all-time peaks; the storefront region is unspecified. See the [historical sources](research/app-store-history.md).
